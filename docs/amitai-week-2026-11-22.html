אתר ודפי נחיתהWebsite and landing pages

  • כל דף מבוסס על כרטיס השירות: „במילים של הלקוח” = הכותרת, „מוצר” = מה מקבלים, „מדד הצלחה” = ההבטחה.
  • טופס הדף מוביל לשאלון ההיכרות באפליקציה ונכנס עם קוד השירות ומקור ה-UTM.
  • מחירים בדף רק לשירותים שעיקרון 7 מגדיר כפומביים (שער כניסה).
  • One landing page per ready service, at an address by code: /services/gl-2.
  • Each page follows the service card: "in the client's words" = the headline, "product" = what they get, "success metric" = the promise.
  • The page's form leads to the app's discovery form and arrives with the service code and UTM source.
  • Prices on a page only for services the 7 principles make public (entry gate).

משימות ותוריםTasks and queues

  • כותרת משימה: פועל + חברה + מה, למשל „לברר: מחזור 2025 – Cortex Dental”.
  • לכל משימה אחראי אחד ותאריך; משימה בלי תאריך היא הערה, לא משימה.
  • Defer יוצר משימת „לחזור” בתאריך שנקבע; Verify יוצר משימת „לבדוק”.
  • מידע חסר בתשובת לקוח הופך למשימה (קיים ב-Release 4).
  • Task title: verb + company + what, e.g. "Find out: 2025 revenue – Cortex Dental".
  • Every task has one owner and a date; a task without a date is a note, not a task.
  • Defer creates a "come back" task on the set date; Verify creates a "check" task.
  • Missing information in a client reply becomes a task (exists since Release 4).

  • Stable IDs: C- company, O- opportunity, D- deal, T- task – used in internal emails and documents.
  • Mandate and service codes in capitals: SH1, GL-2.

כללים סביב הדירוגRules around the score

  • פסילה לפני דירוג: רשימת ההחרגה של SHEPHA, אין יצוא מוכח, אין קשר מזוהה לישראל → Reject, בלי דירוג.
  • Defer הוא החלטה, לא ציון: מפיץ חתום או MoU בשוק → המלצת Defer גם עם דירוג גבוה.
  • „מורידים עדיפות” כחיסור קבוע (הצעה): מחזור עיקרי מחוץ לטווח, החלטה בחו”ל, חברת בת/מפעל/JV בשוק → −10 כל אחד, מינימום 0. מוצג כתגית.
  • מתחת לרצפה (SH1, SH3: 10M$–30M$): תגית בלבד, לא משנה את הציון.
  • סדר התור: קודם כרטיסים עם טריגר, אחר כך לפי ציון. כרטיס בלי טריגר לא נמחק.
  • השערים הישנים (החלטה בישראל, מחזור בטוח, לא פעילה בשוק) מוצגים כמידע ולא נכנסים לחישוב.
  • Disqualify before scoring: the SHEPHA exclusion list, no proven export, no identified Israeli connection → Reject, no score.
  • Defer is a decision, not a score: a signed distributor or MoU in the market → a Defer recommendation even with a high score.
  • "Lowers priority" as a fixed deduction (proposal): main revenue outside the range, decision abroad, a subsidiary/plant/JV in the market → −10 each, minimum 0. Shown as a badge.
  • Below floor (SH1, SH3: $10M–$30M): a badge only, the score is unchanged.
  • Queue order: cards with a trigger first, then by score. A card without a trigger is never deleted.
  • The old gates (decision in Israel, safe revenue, not active in the market) are shown as information and are not part of the calculation.

The agent's workflow

The agent goes through seven steps and stops before outreach. It does not send messages: outreach drafts arrive only in stage 3 of Tom's plan, and every approach remains personal, up to three touches.

  1. Finding. Search by mandate: subsector, size and target market. Two routes in parallel: scanning triggers (trade shows and delegations, jobs, news, distributor pages) and filtering databases by profile.
  2. Checking against the banks. Each name is checked against the companies in the application, the list of rejected ones, the list of the barred and SHEPHA's exclusion list, including former names, parent companies and acquisitions. Stop: already in the bank: add opportunities to the existing card and do not open a duplicate. Rejected in the past: show the old reason; those rejected because of "a decision abroad" are reopened.
  3. Classification. Connection to Israel, sector and subsector, business model, customer type (business / consumer / public), size, ownership, export, fit to each market.
  4. Verification. The company is registered and active in the registrar, the website is live, for revenue and export there is a source, and the company's status in the target market is as described (operates there or not, and if so how). A fact that is not found is recorded as "unknown" and not assumed.
  5. Contact. The person truly responsible for the need: international sales, Asia, business development or growth strategy. Not a group president or the CEO of a large company, unless he owns the need. In a small company the CEO or founder is usually the right person (for example Dror Yamin of Crow). Up to three different people, so that phone, LinkedIn and email can be approached in parallel. The role is verified in the last 30 days and the email passes a validity check.
  6. Recommendation. A mandate, a service from the table above, a reason to act now in one sentence, and a list of what is unknown.
  7. Review queue. The card enters sorted: first those with triggers, then by score. The mandate owner marks Accept, Verify, Defer or Reject with a reason. Accept opens a "Not started" card in the CRM. In the pilot: up to 5 new companies per mandate in each run.

Keywords for finding contacts (by role and by LinkedIn title): VP · VP Marketing & Sales · VP Business Development · Asia-Pacific Market Development · Head of APAC / Asia Sales · International Sales · B2B Sales Leader · Revenue Growth · Global Business Development · Strategic Partnerships. A person whose title mentions B2B sales, revenue growth or business development in Asian markets comes before someone whose role is general. Every service in this document is built on seven rules we have already agreed. A service that does not meet them does not enter the catalogue.

  1. The funnel and the escalation. The core team's business-strategic consulting is the product. Specialists come in only when the assessment shows a need.
  2. Priced as products, not hours. Every service has a defined product the client receives in hand. Hour tracking is internal only.
  3. Every assessment ends in writing. A gap map, action recommendations and a 90-day road map.
  4. One point of contact. The core team passes from the assessment to the specialist, supervises and returns for feedback. The specialist does not replace us towards the client.
  5. Escalation by size. A small business gets a one-off service with a clear product. A larger business gets support. Every one-off service has a defined continuation.
  6. Knowledge, not a pool of suppliers. We decide which specialist, how much and when. The client does not choose from a HUB list.
  7. Opening the skies. The international services (India, sourcing, Asia) are available to a small Israeli client too, and that is what sets us apart.
