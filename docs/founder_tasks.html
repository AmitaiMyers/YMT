Decisions to make (no rush)

  • Signatures: the built-in e-signature is a simple electronic signature. If a contract needs a qualified signature under Israeli law, use a certified signing provider for that contract.
  • Sending email: today you send each approved email from your own mailbox and click "mark as sent". Decide later whether you want a per-message Send button in the app (it would still require approval).
  • Demo data: if you loaded the sample data, delete the "(demo)" companies and resources before going live, or start from an empty database.
